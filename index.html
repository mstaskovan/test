Preliminary Cockpit Preparation

PRELIMINARY AIRCRAFT SETUP
  • 1. RADAR & PRED W/S Also GAIN to CAL, MULTISCAN to AUTO, GCS to AUTO.
    OFF
  • 2. ENG MODE selector NORM
  • 3. ENG 1 & 2 MASTER LEVERS OFF
  • 3. LANDING GEAR lever DOWN
  • 5. BOTH WIPER selectors
    OFF
ELECTRICAL POWER INITIALIZATION
  • 1. BAT 1 & 2 Check voltage (>25.5V).
    Check/AUTO
  • 2. EXT POWER ON (IF AVAIL green lights). Wait until all aircraft systems are up and running (beep beep beep).
    ON
  • 3. APU Deciding whether we need an APU (based on temperature). Set the radio to VHF1 active frequency, VHF2 guard frequency (121.5).
  • APU FIRE TEST pb PRESS and MAINTAIN
  • APU MASTER pb sw Wait 3s before selecting START.
    ON
  • APU START Wait until APU is available.
    ON
  • EXT PWR (After APU Start) The flight crew should keep ON the external power units to reduce the APU load, particularly in hot weather conditions.
    AS RQRD
  • APU BLEED pb sw Before turning on, we wait another 3 minutes from the APU avail status. Meanwhile, we can continue with the next step in this group.
    ON
  • 4. ALL IR MODE selectors To perform an alignment, the aircraft must be stopped on ground.
    NAV
  • COCKPIT LIGHTS Set INT LT, FLOOD LT, INTEG LT (including glareshield and FCU)
    AS RQRD
EFB / FMS PREINITIALIZATION
  • EFB All EFB start. Import flight from siembrief.
    CHECK
  • ACARS ATSU - AOC MENU - FLT INIT. MCDU MENU - FMGC CHECK - engine & aircraft typ, database valid. INIT pg - INIT REQUEST (fill FLT NBR).
    INITIALIZE
ECAM / LOGBOOK CHECK
  • ECAM RCL pb This action recalls all the warnings that the flight crew cleared or cancelled during the last flight.
    PRESS 3s
  • LOGBOOK
    CHECK
  • MEL/CDL items Minimum Equipment List (list of instruments and systems that can be faulty and the aircraft can still take off) / Configuration Deviation List (list of missing parts of the airframe).
    CHECK
  • PRELIM T.O. PERF DATA Check weather and conditions for extra fuel. Compute TOW from the OFP add extra fuel, calculate the Regulated Take Off Weight (RTWO) and compare that to the planned (computed) TOW.
    COMPUTE AND LOAD
  • OEB Operations Engineering Bulletins (deviation or error is detected in the aircraft's software/hardware that is not in accordance with the original manual).
    CHECK
  • AIRCRAFT ACCEPTANCE PERFORM
BEFORE WALKAROUND
  • ECAM pg OXY PRESS, HYD & OIL QTY CHECK
  • CIRCUIT BREAKERS (C/B) Panel CHECK
  • 1. NAV & LOGO LIGHTS ON
  • 2. ACCU PRESS BRAKES CHECK
  • 3. FLAPS Check the upper ECAM display to confirm that the FLAPS position agrees with the lever position.
    CHECK POSITION
  • 4. SPD BRK LEVER CHECK RET
  • 5. PARKING BRAKE ON

OVERHEAD PANEL
  • ALL WHITE LIGHTS EXTINGUISH
  • RCRD GND CTL ON

  • GPWS / TERR pb ON

  • SIGNS SET
  • PROBE/WINDOW HEAT CHECK AUTO
  • LDG ELEV knob AUTO
  • PACK FLOW Select: LO if the number of passengers is 319/320 below 130, HI for abnormally hot and humid conditions, NORM for all other operating cases.
    AS RQRD
  • ELEC BAT Check PRESS ELEC sys SD page then BAT 1 pb sw and BAT 2 pb sw OFF then immediately ON. This action initiates a charging cycle of the batteries. 10 s after setting the BAT pb sw to ON, check on the ELEC SD page that the current charge of the battery is below 60 A and is decreasing.
    AS RQRD
  • FUEL PUMP ON
  • ENG 1 TEST pb and ENG 2 TEST pb Check that all the ENG FIRE detection and extinguishing systems are operative.
    PRESS and MAINTAIN
